## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛡️ Smart TaxShield — Privacy-Preserving Multimodal AI for Tax Fraud Detection

An academic graduation project exploring how AI can support tax-fraud risk analysis while protecting sensitive data.

- Combines machine learning, large language models, retrieval-based methods, and multimodal AI within a privacy-focused framework.
- Explores Federated Learning and Differential Privacy to support privacy-preserving analysis.
- Integrates tax-document understanding, evidence retrieval, and AI-assisted risk assessment workflows.

**Technologies:** Python, Machine Learning, LLMs, RAG, CAG, Federated Learning, Differential Privacy, Multimodal AI.

### 🎙️ Arabic Audio Understanding & Retrieval System

An NLP project focused on processing Arabic audio and enabling information retrieval from transcribed content.

- Uses speech recognition and NLP components to support transcription, summarization, and semantic retrieval.
- Incorporates vector search to retrieve relevant content from processed audio and text.

**Technologies:** Python, Whisper, Transformers, FAISS, NLP.

### 💬 AI Food Delivery Chatbot

A conversational application designed to support food-delivery interactions and connect chatbot workflows with backend services.

- Integrates conversational interactions with APIs and a database-backed application.

**Technologies:** Python, Dialogflow, FastAPI, MySQL, REST APIs.
